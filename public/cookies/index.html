Last updated: 30 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how drilianavaliullina.com uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with the Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels used by some third-party tools. They can be essential for a site to work, or used for analytics and advertising.

2. What this website uses

This website does not set advertising or analytics tracking cookies (no Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, or similar marketing trackers are installed on these pages).

Name / type Purpose Duration
site-theme (localStorage) Remembers light/dark theme preference Until cleared by you
cookie-notice-ack (localStorage) Remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice Until cleared by you
Hosting / CDN (e.g. Cloudflare) Security, performance, and delivery of the site As set by the provider

3. Third-party tools

  • Google Fonts — fonts may be loaded from Google. Google may process technical data such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Cal.com embed — the booking calendar on the lessons page loads Cal.com. Cal.com (and related payment providers such as Stripe) may set their own cookies when you interact with booking or payment. See Cal.com’s and Stripe’s policies.

Instagram / Meta ads you see on social platforms are controlled by Meta under Meta’s own settings and policies; those ads are not the same as placing a Meta tracking pixel on this website.

4. Your choices

  • You can clear site data / cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can use browser controls to block third-party cookies (this may affect booking embeds).
  • If marketing or analytics cookies are added to this site in future, this policy will be updated and a clearer consent choice will be provided where required.
